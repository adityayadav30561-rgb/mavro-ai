- Heavy Maintenance Cost
- Legacy ERP annual license and maintenance fees are significant and rise each renewal cycle. - REASON 03
- Forced Version Migration
- Legacy platforms reach end-of-life on the vendor's schedule. A version migration then becomes mandatory — a full platform upgrade with new implementation costs, timeline disruption and re-training. ❯ Mainstream maintenance ends on a fixed date ❯ Mandatory upgrade = a fresh implementation ❯ Why pay for another migration when MAVRO covers every gap now?
