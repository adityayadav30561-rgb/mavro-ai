## Slide 3

- Common Transport Gaps in Manufacturing Businesses
- Registers and phone calls move the truck — but lanes, freight cost and delivery proof fall back to manual workarounds
- 🖥️ Manual Dispatch & WhatsApp Coordination — A Foundation With Gaps
- Registers record the LR — but leave lane rates, in-transit status, delivery proof and freight bills untracked and disputable
- 💰 MANUAL EFFORT
- ❌ Hours spent on indents, follow-ups & bill checking ❌ Effort rises with volume — accuracy does not ❌ Planners tied to the phone instead of the plan
- 🧩 DISCONNECTED TOOLS
- ❌ Orders, GPS app and freight billing in separate places ❌ Data re-keyed by hand between systems ❌ No single trip record anyone can trust
- ⚠️ COMPLIANCE RISK
- ❌ E-way bills raised outside the system, at the portal ❌ Vehicle and driver documents checked on paper ❌ Any lapse means detention and penalty
- GAP: DISPATCH & TRANSPORTER DISCONNECT
- 📊 Dispatch & Transporter Disconnect
- ❌ Orders grouped into loads on a spreadsheet
- ❌ Indents raised on calls — placement unconfirmed
- ❌ Allocation on habit, not on rate or reliability
- ❌ Spot rates agreed verbally, reconciled much later
- Result: trucks booked without a costed plan
- GAP: IN-TRANSIT VISIBILITY
- 🚚 In-Transit Visibility Gap
- ❌ Trip status known only when the driver answers
- ❌ Delays discovered at the customer's gate
- ❌ Unplanned halts and diversions never recorded
- ❌ Driving hours and rest breaks entirely untracked
- Result: the customer knows before you do
- GAP: FREIGHT COST & PROOF
- 🧾 Freight Cost & Proof Gap
- ❌ Bills passed without matching the rate contract
- ❌ Delivery proof on paper — claims unwinnable
- ❌ Detention and short-delivery settled by argument
- ❌ Freight cost known only when the bill arrives
- Result: leakage nobody can quantify
