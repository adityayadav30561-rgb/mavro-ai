## Slide 4

- Why Businesses Move Off Legacy ERP
- Three structural reasons driving the move to a modern, purpose-built platform
- 💰
- REASON 01
- Heavy Maintenance Cost
- Legacy ERP annual license and maintenance fees are significant and rise each renewal cycle. You pay heavily for a system that still requires manual workarounds in HR, channel and production. ❯ High license + maintenance fees every year ❯ Cost grows without added capability ❯ No flexibility to scale down or pause
- Financial burden with no clear ceiling
- 🧩
- REASON 02
- Missing Modules & Add-On Dependency
- Legacy ERP often lacks native Retailer App, Vendor Portal and mobile — and India statutory payroll (PF/ESI/TDS) requires a separate add-on. Each carries its own cost, support risk and integration complexity. ❯ Retailer App, Vendor Portal — not native ❯ Statutory payroll needs a partner add-on ❯ Multiple partners = fragmented support
- Dependent on partners — no single owner
- ⚠️
- REASON 03
- Forced Version Migration
- Legacy platforms reach end-of-life on the vendor's schedule. A version migration then becomes mandatory — a full platform upgrade with new implementation costs, timeline disruption and re-training. ❯ Mainstream maintenance ends on a fixed date ❯ Mandatory upgrade = a fresh implementation ❯ Why pay for another migration when MAVRO covers every gap now?
- Switch now — before the next migration is forced on you
